How much does Starlink cost in Malaysia?

A plain breakdown of what you pay for Starlink in Malaysia: the kit, the monthly plan and installation, with a first-year estimate.

Check availability WhatsApp us

Starlink pricing has three parts: a one-off payment for the hardware kit, a monthly plan, and installation if you don't want to mount it yourself. Starlink sets the hardware and plan prices; we quote installation.

Prices below are as reported by Lowyat.NET and LepakLah in mid-2026. Starlink changes prices from time to time, so always confirm on starlink.com before ordering.

1. The hardware kit

KitPriceBest for
StandardRM1,600Homes, longhouses and businesses with a fixed location
MiniRM930Portable use and small sites with light usage

The Standard kit includes the dish and a Wi-Fi router. For a fixed home, longhouse or business, it's the one we usually recommend: it has a wider field of view and copes better with heavy use.

2. The monthly plan

PlanPrice per monthSpeed
Residential 100RM135Up to 100Mbps
ResidentialRM235Up to 400Mbps, unlimited data
BusinessFrom RM217Priority data for business use
RoamFrom RM220For use on the move

Residential 100 replaced the older Residential Lite plan in 2026. For a household, Residential 100 is usually enough. For a longhouse with many families, a school or a business, the full Residential or a Business plan is the safer choice. We'll help you pick based on how many people will use it.

3. Installation

Starlink is designed so you can set it up yourself, and on an open site that can work. In rural Sarawak, professional installation is usually worth it because of:

  • Trees and obstructions. Even partial blockage causes dropouts. We find the mounting spot, or pole height, that gives a clear view of the sky.
  • Secure mounting. A properly fixed roof or pole mount stays aligned through storms and strong wind.
  • Cabling and weatherproofing. Neat, sealed cable runs that won't let water into the house.
  • Wi-Fi coverage. Large houses and longhouses need extra access points so the signal reaches every room.
  • Power. Advice on running Starlink from generator or solar power.

Our installation price depends on the mounting type, cable length, extra Wi-Fi equipment and travel to your site. Send us photos on WhatsApp and we'll give you a quote before any work is booked.

Guide FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Message us on WhatsApp

Is there a contract for Starlink in Malaysia?

Starlink plans are billed monthly. Check the current terms on starlink.com when you order, as they can change.

Is installation included in the Starlink price?

No. Starlink's kit price covers the hardware only. You can install it yourself, or we can install it for a quoted fee.

Which plan should I choose for a longhouse?

It depends on how many people will be online at once. For many families sharing one connection, the full Residential plan is the safer choice. See Starlink for longhouses.
