Starlink installation in Miri

Satellite internet across Miri Division, from the city and the coast at Bekenu to Niah, Sibuti and the gateway to the Baram.

Check availability WhatsApp us

Who we install for around Miri

Outside the city, much of Miri Division depends on long stretches of road with weak mobile coverage. We install Starlink for:

  • Homes and longhouses along the coastal road and inland around Niah, Sibuti and Beluru.
  • Plantation estates, worker quarters and site offices that need dependable connectivity away from town.
  • Tourism operators near the national parks who need Wi-Fi for guests and online bookings.

Gateway to the Baram

Miri is the starting point for most of our work further inland. For longhouses along the Baram, around Marudi, Long Lama and beyond, we plan the journey with you, whether by road or river. Read more on our Baram page.

How installation works in Miri

Message us your location with photos of the roof and surroundings. We'll confirm the right kit and plan, then book a technician. Sites close to Miri are usually visited within 24–48 hours of confirming your order; remote sites are scheduled around travel.

Starlink in Miri FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Starlink work in Niah and Sibuti?

Yes. Starlink works anywhere with a clear view of the sky. In plantation areas we check for tall trees around the mounting point and use a pole mount where needed.

Can you connect a plantation or several estates around Miri?

Yes. We install and look after multi-site setups and monitor every site from the Desalink Terminal. See Starlink for business.

How much does Starlink cost in Miri?

Hardware and plan prices are set by Starlink and are the same across Malaysia. See our Starlink price guide. Installation is quoted per site.
