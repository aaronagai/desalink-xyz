Who we install for around Kuching

Kuching city has good fibre, but coverage thins out quickly once you leave the main roads. We install Starlink for:

  • Homes and kampung in Padawan, Bau, Lundu and Serian where fibre hasn't arrived and mobile data is patchy.
  • Homestays, farms and resorts on the coast and in the hills that need reliable Wi-Fi for guests and bookings.
  • Shops and offices in town that want a backup connection when their fibre line goes down.

Why Starlink works well here

Starlink only needs a clear view of the sky, so it isn't limited by how far you are from the nearest exchange or tower. Hilly ground around Padawan and Bau that blocks mobile signal doesn't matter to a dish pointed straight up.

The main thing we check on site is tree cover. Tall trees around a house are the most common cause of interruptions, and part of our job is finding the mounting spot, or pole height, that clears them.

How installation works in Kuching

Send us your location and a few photos of your roof and surroundings on WhatsApp. We'll recommend the right kit and plan, then book a technician. Most sites around Kuching are visited within 24–48 hours of confirming your order, and the installation itself takes 2–4 hours.

Do I need Starlink if I live in Kuching city?

If you already have fibre that works well, usually not. Starlink makes most sense on the edges of town and in rural districts where fibre isn't available, or as a backup line for a business that can't afford to go offline.

Can you install Starlink at a homestay or resort near Kuching?

Yes. We size the Wi-Fi to cover guest rooms and common areas, not just the main building. For larger sites, see Starlink for business.

How much does Starlink cost in Kuching?

Hardware and plan prices are the same everywhere in Malaysia. See our Starlink price guide. Installation is quoted after a site check, based on mounting and cable run.
